    PNG carries an alpha channel. A browser canvas is always RGBA, so a PNG written from it keeps a transparency channel even when every pixel is fully opaque. Apple rejects App Store screenshots that contain an alpha channel, and Google Play asks for 24-bit PNG with no alpha. JPEG has no alpha channel at all, which is why it is the default here.

    Your screenshot is scaled to fit and never stretched — the aspect ratio you captured is always preserved, and the background fills whatever is left over.
