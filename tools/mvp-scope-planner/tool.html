How the scoring works

  • Every feature scores impact ÷ effort (Low/Medium/High = 1/2/3, Small/Medium/Large = 1/2/3). Higher scores ship sooner.
  • A feature checked “Required to launch” skips scoring entirely and always lands in the MVP bucket.
  • The suggested tier totals the effort points of everything in your MVP bucket and maps that to our own pricing - it is a starting point, not a quote.
